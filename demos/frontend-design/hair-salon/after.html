Reading

We test before we mix

A strand test and a look at your ends tell us more than any photo. It is how we know whether your hair will carry the change in one sitting.

Lifting

Two visits beat one

Going more than two stops in a single appointment risks the condition. We would rather build the colour across two visits than rush it once.

Keeping

The gloss does the work

A ten-minute gloss between full colours is what keeps a level looking fresh. It is the least expensive appointment on our list.

The studio

Every head of hair gets a card.

At the basin we write down your level, the formula we mixed, the developer volume and how long it took to lift. It goes on file under your name and stays there.

It means a correction can be planned instead of guessed at, and a root retouch takes less time on the second visit than it did on the first. Your colour becomes a record, not an experiment.

Good to know

Before you book

The questions we answer most often at the chair. If yours is not here, ask it in the booking form.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for any first-time colour. It takes five minutes, it is free, and it has to happen at least 48 hours before your appointment.

Can you fix a box dye or a colour from elsewhere?

Usually, yes. Send a photo with your request and mention what was used and when. Corrections are quoted after a consultation and a strand test, before we begin.

How often should I rebook?

It depends on your level. Darker levels stretch to six or eight weeks; blondes and platinum usually want a gloss every four weeks and a full visit every eight.

What products do you use?

A professional colour line and a bond-building range for aftercare. We sell the two or three things we actually use, and nothing else.

How do I get there, and is there parking?

We are on 42 Mayor Street, a three-minute walk from Opera and Sol. The nearest public car park is in Plaza Mayor, and there is step-free access at the door.
