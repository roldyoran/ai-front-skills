About

A studio, not just any salon

We were born in 2013 with a simple idea: to reclaim time for yourself. At Studio Aurea there is no rush, there is conversation, coffee and expert hands that listen before cutting.

We are part of a sustainable beauty program: we work with vegan, cruelty-free brands with recyclable packaging.
